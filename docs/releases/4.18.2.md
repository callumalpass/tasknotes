# TaskNotes 4.18.2

## Fixed

- (#2540) Fixed ICS exports for tasks with a due date but no scheduled date. Date-only deadlines now produce an all-day event on the due date; timed deadlines produce a one-hour event starting at the due time. Single-task downloads previously could omit a start date, while exported feeds could stretch the event from its creation date to the deadline. See [Automatic ICS export](https://tasknotes.dev/settings/integrations/#automatic-ics-export).
  - This change applies to `.ics` downloads and feeds, not Google, Outlook or Yahoo calendar links.
  - Thanks to @jayhemnani9910 for diagnosing and fixing this.
